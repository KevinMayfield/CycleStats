Industry and Work

What a town did for a living still shows in how its people travel. The old coalfield and steel towns cycle well below what their hills predict, even when deprivation, car ownership, region and the conurbations around them are taken into account. And COVID-19, whose death rates look a little like a mirror image of cycling, turns out to follow age, the coalfields, deprivation and how far people travel to work; once those are allowed for, towns where more people cycle still had somewhat fewer deaths.

COVID-19: cycling, or what towns did for work?

Deaths registered due to COVID-19 in 2020 and 2021 for each town, per 100,000 residents (ONS, by neighbourhood). Towns where more people cycle had fewer deaths, but those towns also differ in age, industry, deprivation and commuting.
